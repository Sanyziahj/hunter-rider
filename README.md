# HUNTER RIDER: VOID ANOMALIES

A free 16-bit gothic mystery platformer in the browser: https://sanyziahj.github.io/hunter-rider/

The Hunter Rider takes the case of the Void Anomalies. Crack an electric whip (diagonal and grapple swing), fire a laser raygun, and summon Icy once per stage to freeze everything on screen. Examine paintings and objects, collect clues, and solve deduction puzzles from the Hall of Mirrors and the SR Space Diner through Gutter Alley, the Sewers and the Underworld. Defeat the Red Devil Rider, choose who to follow, decide the Wizard's fate, and face Fright and the Overlord before the DreamLand Carnival ending. Spike is your guide. Let's Ride!

Controls: keyboard (arrows/WASD, SPACE jump, J whip, K shoot, L Icy, C case notes, ENTER pause), gamepads, and touch. Saves automatically, so you can continue later.
